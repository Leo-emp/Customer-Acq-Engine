The Agents

Each agent is a specialist. Together they are your autonomous sales team.

🔍

Scout

Lead search via Apollo.io, AI scoring 0-100, competitor analysis, industry research, market mapping. PDF exports.

✉

Reach

Drafts unique cold emails per lead referencing pain points and tech stack. Sends via Gmail API or Resend fallback.

🛡

QA

Scans every email for personalization, tone, compliance. 4 verdict levels from approved to flagged.

📞

Closer

Real outbound AI voice calls via Twilio. Full conversation handling, objection management, transcript analysis.

📄

Invoice

Generates professional invoices with AI line items from deal context. Downloadable PDF export.

Brand Brain — centralized company identity extracted from any URL. Feeds 25+ profile fields to all 5 agents.
